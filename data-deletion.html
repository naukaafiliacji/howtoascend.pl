Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 12 July 2026

Dropshipping How To Ascend P.S.A. respects your right to request deletion of personal data associated with your interactions with our services.

HOW TO REQUEST DATA DELETION

To request deletion of your personal data, send an email to:

contact@dropshippinghowtoascend.com

Use the subject line:

Data Deletion Request

In your message, please provide sufficient information to allow us to identify the data associated with your request.

Depending on how you contacted us, this may include:

  • your name;
  • the telephone number used to contact us through WhatsApp;
  • the email address used to contact us.

Please do not send passwords, access tokens, identification document numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

IDENTITY VERIFICATION

We may request additional information where reasonably necessary to verify that the person submitting the request is authorised to request deletion of the relevant data.

WHAT DATA MAY BE DELETED

Depending on the services you used and subject to applicable law, deletion may include personal data stored in our systems in connection with:

  • communications with us;
  • WhatsApp conversation records processed by our systems;
  • contact information;
  • conversation context or stored user information;
  • training-related communication records;
  • other personal data associated with your interaction with our services.

PROCESSING YOUR REQUEST

After receiving and verifying a valid deletion request, we will process the request in accordance with applicable data protection law.

We will delete or anonymise personal data covered by the request unless we are required or permitted to retain specific information for legal, regulatory, security, fraud prevention, or legal claims purposes.

We may confirm completion of the request using the contact information associated with the request.

THIRD-PARTY PLATFORMS

Deleting information from our systems does not necessarily delete information independently controlled by third-party platforms that you use, including WhatsApp or Meta.

Requests concerning data independently processed by third-party platforms should be submitted directly to the relevant platform in accordance with its privacy procedures.

CONTACT

For questions concerning data deletion or privacy, contact:

Dropshipping How To Ascend P.S.A.

Email:

contact@dropshippinghowtoascend.com
